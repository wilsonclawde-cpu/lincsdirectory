Pet boarding and kennels in Skegness: the basics

What the job involves

Kennels and catteries board pets while you are away; home boarders look after dogs in their own home. All must be licensed by the local council in England and the licence star rating is a useful guide. Visit beforehand and check the pens, exercise areas and how often animals are checked.

Safety and good to know

Boarding without a licence is an offence in England; a licensed business should be happy to show it. LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote.

What vaccinations do kennels require?

Most require up-to-date core vaccinations and many also ask for kennel cough vaccination a couple of weeks before boarding. Check the kennel's policy when you book.
