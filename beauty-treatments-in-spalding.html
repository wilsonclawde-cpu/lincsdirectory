Beauty treatments in Spalding: the basics

What the job involves

Beauty salons and mobile therapists offer waxing, threading, brow and lash tinting and lamination, lash extensions, facials, spray tans and make-up for occasions. Some treatments need a patch test a day or two before the first appointment.

Safety and good to know

Tell the therapist about skin conditions, medication or pregnancy, which can affect which treatments are suitable. LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote.
