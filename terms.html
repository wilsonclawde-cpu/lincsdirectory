Premium listing: what you are buying

A Premium listing costs £4.99, paid once. There is no subscription and no renewal fee. It includes:

  • your own profile page on LincsDirectory with a photo of your business, a prominent link to your website, your phone number, opening hours and a description, all supplied by you;
  • placement at the top of search results for your town and category, marked with a "Premium" tag, for as long as the directory operates and the listing stays accurate;
  • one free local business report from Aide, our business-reports service, delivered by email.

Premium listings are paid placements and appear first. They are always marked so readers can tell. Being Premium does not change a business's ratings, and we do not alter, hide or remove ratings because a business has paid.

We email you a secure payment link; your listing goes Premium once payment clears and we have your details, usually within two working days. We may ask you to change a photo or wording that is unlawful, misleading or unsuitable. If a business closes or asks to be removed, the Premium listing ends with it. If we ever close the directory, we will give Premium businesses at least 30 days' notice; the £4.99 is a one-off payment for the service already provided and is not refunded in that case unless the listing has been live for less than 3 months, in which case we refund it.
