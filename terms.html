Job requests and introductions

LincsDirectory is a directory and an introducer. When you send a "Get local help" request we pass it, with your consent, to listed businesses that say they cover your area and do that kind of work. A request sent from a business's own listing ("Request a quote") comes to LincsDirectory first; we pass it to the business named on the form and, if that business cannot help or does not reply, we may pass it to other listed businesses that cover your area, as the consent you give on the form allows. Businesses receive requests from us by email, marked "Lead from LincsDirectory"; we aim to pass them on within one working day but do not promise to. We are not a party to any contract you then make with a business, we do not carry out, supervise or guarantee any work, and we do not vet, license, inspect or endorse the businesses listed. Being listed, being shown first as a Premium listing, or being sent your request is not a recommendation. Check credentials yourself (for example that a gas engineer is on the Gas Safe Register), ask for references and insurance, and get a written quote before work starts. We make no promise about whether, how many or how quickly businesses will respond. The guidance on our job pages is general information written from public sources; it is not advice for your situation and is not medical, legal or financial advice.

Businesses that list with us agree that any credentials they give are accurate and theirs to use, that they will contact people only about the request they were sent, and that they will handle the details lawfully. We may remove a listing or stop passing requests to a business at any time, including where we receive complaints.

Promoted listing: what you are buying

A Promoted listing (shown on the site with the "Premium" tag) costs £2.49 a month, VAT included, billed monthly in advance by subscription through Revolut (see Payments for who bills you). There is no minimum term and no commission. It includes:

  • your own profile page on LincsDirectory with your logo, a photo gallery of up to 8 images with captions, a prominent link to your website, your phone number, opening hours, a full description (up to 2,000 characters, in paragraphs) and up to 20 service points (up to 80 characters each, no prices), all supplied by you; your search card and your rows on town and job pages show the logo, one line and the first 3 service points with a “See full profile” link;
  • placement at the top of search results and job pages for your town, category and jobs, marked with a "Premium" tag, for as long as your subscription is paid and the listing stays accurate;
  • for service-area businesses, cover for as many Lincolnshire towns as you serve, or the whole county (a free listing covers its home area, up to 3 towns);
  • one free local business report from Aide, our business-reports service, delivered by email when your subscription starts.

Premium listings are paid placements and appear first. They are always marked so readers can tell. Being Premium does not change a business's ratings, and we do not alter, hide or remove ratings because a business has paid.

Billing. You subscribe from the For businesses or claim page through Revolut's secure checkout (card or Revolut Pay). The first £2.49 is taken when you subscribe and the same amount on the same date each month after that, until you cancel. The checkout asks for your name and email only: give the same email on our form so we can match the payment to your listing. Your listing is switched to Promoted by a person, within one working day of us seeing the payment, once we have your details (logo, photos and text come by email; we resize photos and there is no upload form). You must hold the rights to any logo or photo you send and allow us to show it on the site. We may ask you to change a photo or wording that is unlawful, misleading or unsuitable, and we may decline images that are not of your business or your work. If a business closes or asks to be removed, the Promoted listing ends with it.

Cancelling. You can cancel at any time from the link in Revolut's receipt or subscription emails, through the form, by email to hello@lincsdirectory.co.uk or by phone on 01522 424963. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you have already paid for: the listing stays Promoted until then and then goes back to a free listing (name, address or the home area of up to 3 towns, category, map pin, one-line description, up to 3 service bullets, a short description of up to 400 characters, and ratings). We do not refund part of a month unless the law requires it. If a payment fails and is not made within 14 days, the listing goes back to free until you subscribe again.

Ratings policy

  • Moderated. Every rating is read by a person before it appears. Nothing is published automatically, and we never invent ratings, reviews or counts.
  • Genuine opinions only. By sending a rating you confirm you have used the business and that it is your honest opinion. Do not rate your own business, a competitor's, or one you have been asked or paid to rate.
  • No incentives, no paid removal. We do not pay for ratings or offer rewards for them, and we do not accept payment from anyone to add, change or remove a rating. Premium status makes no difference to ratings.
  • What we may remove. We may decline or remove any rating that is abusive, defamatory, discriminatory, off-topic, contains personal data about someone else, looks fake or automated, or breaks the law. We may shorten a comment to fit; we do not change its meaning.
  • Right of reply. A business can reply to a rating, or ask us to review one, through the claim form. Replies are moderated in the same way and shown under the rating.
  • Your rating, your choice. You can ask us to remove a rating you sent at any time. How we handle the details you give us is in the privacy notice.
