Featured listing: what you are buying

A Featured listing costs £4.99, paid once. There is no subscription and no renewal fee. It includes:

  • a photo of your business, a link to your website, your phone number, opening hours and a short description, all supplied by you;
  • placement at the top of search results for your town and category, with a clear "Featured" label, for as long as the directory operates and the listing stays accurate;
  • one free local business report from Aide, our business-reports service, delivered by email.

We email you a secure payment link; your listing goes Featured once payment clears and we have your details, usually within two working days. We may ask you to change a photo or wording that is unlawful, misleading or unsuitable. If a business closes or asks to be removed, the Featured listing ends with it. If we ever close the directory, we will give Featured businesses at least 30 days' notice; the £4.99 is a one-off payment for the service already provided and is not refunded in that case unless the listing has been live for less than 3 months, in which case we refund it.
