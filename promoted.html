1

Top of the results

In search results, on the job pages and on the town and category pages, Promoted listings come before free ones, with the Premium tag so readers can tell. If more than one listing is Promoted, the better match comes first, then A to Z.

You appear wherever your listing would appear anyway: the job pages for the work you do (your category) and the towns you cover, your town's page, your category's page and the search page. Promoted doesn't put you on pages for jobs or towns that aren't yours. The one list that isn't ordered this way is the home page's "Nearest to you", which is by distance.

Promoted listings are paid placements and are always marked; see the terms.

2

Cover more than your local area

Work from home or travel to the job? We show the towns you cover instead of an address. A free listing covers your home area, up to 3 towns. Promoted covers as many towns as you serve, or the whole county.

Each town you cover puts you on that town's page, on its job pages for the work you do (for example "Boiler service in Grantham") and in searches for that town. Choose the whole county and your listing says "Covers all of Lincolnshire" and appears for every one of the 22 towns. You still only appear for your own category and jobs, and Promoted listings still come first.

Tell us the towns on the sign-up or claim form; we never publish a home address.

3

Customer ratings

Anyone can rate any business from its listing, free or Promoted, and a person reads every rating before it appears. We never invent ratings or counts.

Being Promoted doesn't change your ratings and doesn't buy a better score. What it adds is that your ratings and the latest approved comments show on your own profile page, next to your photo, description and website link, where a customer can act on them. You can reply to a rating through the claim form.

No customer ratings have been published yet, so every listing currently shows “Be the first to rate”. Ratings policy.

A clear "Visit website" button on your profile page and your search card, so people who like what they see go straight to you.

Phone, opening hours and a longer description

Your phone number (tappable on a mobile), your opening hours and a longer description in your own words (up to 1,200 characters, with up to 8 service bullets), on your profile page and your search card.

A free listing you add or claim yourself carries one line about your business (up to 100 characters), up to 3 service bullets and a short description of about five lines (up to 400 characters) under “More about this business”; listings built from public records carry none of this. We never add phone numbers, hours or descriptions unless the business gives them to us. Credentials you mention are shown as supplied by you, never as verified, and readers are told to check before they book.

Questions

Straight answers

How do I get started?

Send the sign-up form on the For businesses page (business name, phone, website and email), choose Promoted, then press Subscribe. A person reads your website, works out your category, the jobs you do and the towns you cover, and builds your listing. Already in the directory? Use the claim form instead and choose Promoted.

How do I pay?

By monthly subscription through Revolut's secure checkout: card or Revolut Pay, £2.49 a month (VAT included). The checkout asks only for your name and email, so use the same email address as on the form; that is how we match your payment to your listing. We never take card details by phone or email.

How quickly is it switched on?

Within one working day of us seeing your payment. Nothing is automated: a person matches the payment to your form by email, adds your photo, website, phone, hours and description, publishes your profile page and emails you when it's live. Your free Aide report follows by email.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time: from the link in Revolut's receipt emails, through the claim form, or by emailing hello@lincsdirectory.co.uk. There is no minimum term. Your listing stays Promoted until the end of the month you've paid for, then goes back to free; your name, area, category and ratings stay. We don't refund part of a month unless the law requires it, and we give at least 30 days' notice of any price change.

What if I'm not in the directory yet?

Join through the For businesses form. We create your free listing first (usually within a few days) and, if you chose Promoted, switch the extras on once your payment shows. You can also start free and upgrade later through the claim form.

How many towns can I cover?

If you work from home or travel to the job we show the towns you cover instead of an address. A free listing covers your home area, up to 3 towns. A Promoted listing covers as many of the 22 Lincolnshire towns as you serve, or the whole county (shown as “Covers all of Lincolnshire”), and appears on each of those towns' pages and job pages for your category. Tell us the towns on the sign-up or claim form; you can change them any time.

What does the free listing show?

Your business name, your category, your address or the towns you cover (up to 3), a pin on the map, customer ratings, and, when you add or claim the listing yourself, one line about your business (up to 100 characters), up to 3 service bullets and a short description of about five lines (up to 400 characters), all in your words and read by us before they appear, an “Added by the business” tag and an Update details link. Listings built from public records show the name, address and category only until the business claims them.

How do customer requests reach me?

By email. When someone sends a Get local help request for a job you do in a town you cover, or presses Request a quote on your listing, the request comes to LincsDirectory first. A person reads it and passes it to you as an email marked “Lead from LincsDirectory”, with the customer's details and what they need. We aim to pass requests on within one working day. We can't promise how many you'll get, and being listed is not a recommendation; the customer deals with you directly.
