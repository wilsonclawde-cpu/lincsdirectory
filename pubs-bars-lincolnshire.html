About this page

How many pubs and bars are listed in Lincolnshire?

169 listings are live today across the 22 town areas we cover, out of 687 on our schedule. The list refreshes from the live data every day.

How are businesses put in the pubs & bars category?

From the business type recorded in the public data (the Food Standards Agency business type, or the OpenStreetMap shop, amenity or craft tag), mapped to one of our 36 categories, plus name-based rules for things like play centres and bowling alleys that the public data files under restaurants. If a business is in the wrong category, it can claim the listing and tell us, free.

Where does this list come from?

Names, addresses and categories come from the Food Standards Agency's Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (Open Government Licence v3.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). We publish nothing else unless the business gives it to us.
