Kids' party venue or entertainer: the basics

What the job involves

Children's parties can be held at soft play centres, trampoline parks, leisure centres, village halls or at home with an entertainer, bouncy castle or mobile activity. Venues often offer packages with food and a party host; entertainers bring games, magic, face painting or characters.

What to ask a pro, and what to have ready

Have the date, age group and numbers. Ask what the package includes and for how long, the price per child and for adults, deposit and cancellation terms, and for entertainers, whether they have public liability insurance. Hired inflatables should have a safety test certificate and supervision.

Safety and good to know

Good to know

ask the venue about allergies and whether you can bring your own cake. Soft play venues often have age limits for different areas and a cap on numbers. An entertainer who is used to the age group will keep things moving; check reviews from parents and how long they have been performing.
