Repair or replace windows in Market Deeping: the basics

What the job involves

Misted double glazing means the sealed unit has failed; it can be replaced in the existing frame. Handles, hinges and locks on uPVC windows are standard parts a window repairer can swap. A new window installation includes removing the old frame, fitting and sealing the new one and making good inside.

Safety and good to know

Replacement windows must meet Building Regulations in England; a registered installer self-certifies and you should receive a certificate. Yes. The sealed glass unit is replaced in the existing frame, which is much cheaper than a new window, as long as the frame itself is sound.
