Questions

Straight answers

Is my business already listed?

Probably, if you run a food business or a shop with a public address in Lincolnshire. We build the directory from public records: the Food Standards Agency's Food Hygiene Rating Scheme and OpenStreetMap. Search for your business name; if it is there, you can claim it and take control of the details. If it is not, add it free.

What does a free listing include?

Your business name, address, category and a pin on the map. That is all we publish from public data. Claim the listing to correct the name, address or category at no cost.

What does the Premium listing cost and include?

£4.99, paid once. You get your own profile page with a photo, a link to your website, your phone number, opening hours and a description, you appear first in search results for your town and category with a Premium tag, and we send you a free local business report from Aide.

How do I pay?

Ask for a Premium listing using the claim form and we will email you a secure payment link. We do not take card details by phone or email.

Are the ratings real?

Yes. Anyone can rate a business from its listing. A person reads every rating before it appears, we never invent ratings or counts, and businesses can reply through the claim form. Where a business has no approved ratings yet we say so.

Can I suggest a business that isn't listed?

Yes. Use the Suggest a business page: give us the name and the town, and anything else you know. We check each suggestion against public records before it goes live, usually within a few days. Nothing is published automatically.

How do I remove my listing?

Use the Remove my listing option on the claim form or phone 01522 424963. We act on removal requests promptly and add the business to our do-not-list record so it does not come back with the next data refresh.

Where does the information come from?

Names, addresses and categories come from the Food Standards Agency (Open Government Licence v3.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), both public datasets. Where the OpenStreetMap record includes a business phone number we show it; otherwise we do not publish phone numbers, emails or opening hours unless the business gives them to us. Details on Premium listings come from the business itself.

Do you check or recommend the tradespeople listed?

No. LincsDirectory is a directory and an introducer, not a contractor or a vetting service. We don't vet or endorse businesses, so always check credentials (Gas Safe registration for gas work, proof of electrical registration, insurance) and get a written quote before work starts. Our job pages tell you what to ask.

What happens when I send a 'Get local help' request?

A person at LincsDirectory reads it and passes it, with the contact details you gave, to listed local businesses that cover your area and do that kind of work. They may contact you by phone or email. We only share your request with the consent you give on the form, and we keep it for up to 12 months.

Who runs LincsDirectory?

TAG Sleaford Ltd, a Lincolnshire company based at Kesteven Business Centre in Sleaford. We also run the business centre and Aide, our business-reports service, and both have Premium listings on this site. Premium listings are paid placements and are always marked.
