About Example Plumbing Co.

Example Plumbing Co. is a made-up business used to show what a Promoted profile looks like when every part of it is filled in. Nothing on this page is real: the name, the phone number, the website and the service list are placeholders, and the grey blocks stand in for a logo and photos.

A real description goes here, in the business's own words: what you do, how long you have been doing it, the kind of jobs you take on and the areas you cover. Up to 2,000 characters in as many paragraphs as you like, so there is room to explain how you work, what a customer should have ready, and what happens after they get in touch.

A second or third paragraph might cover the practical side: how quickly you can usually come out, whether you quote in writing, which payment methods you take and what guarantees you give. Credentials you mention are shown as supplied by you, never as verified by LincsDirectory, and readers are told to check before they book.

Below the description come your service points (up to 20), your photos (up to 8, with captions), the towns you cover (or all of Lincolnshire), your opening hours and your customer ratings.
