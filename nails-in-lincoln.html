Nails and manicure in Lincoln: the basics

What the job involves

Nail salons offer manicures and pedicures, gel polish, acrylic or builder-gel extensions, nail art and removal. Gel and acrylic need professional removal to avoid damaging the natural nail. Mobile nail technicians visit your home.

Safety and good to know

If you have had a reaction to gel products before, tell the technician; allergies to some ingredients are increasingly common. LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote.
