Suggest a business

Know a great local business? Tell us.

If a Lincolnshire business is missing from the directory, give us the name and the town. We'll check it against public records and add it free. You don't need an account and you don't have to leave your details.

Who are you?

What happens next

  • We check the business exists and trades from a public address in Lincolnshire, using public records.
  • We add a free listing: name, address, category and a map pin. Nothing else unless the business gives it to us.
  • Usually within a few days. If you left an email, we'll tell you when it's live.
  • The business can claim, correct or remove its listing at any time.
Your own business?

Claim it instead

If it's your business, the claim form lets you fix details, or go Premium for £4.99 once: your own profile page with photo, website, phone and hours, first in results and a free Aide business report.
