About this page

How many restaurants are listed in Lincolnshire?

27 listings are live today across the 22 town areas we cover, out of 865 on our schedule. The list refreshes from the live data every day.

How are businesses put in the restaurants category?

From the business type recorded in the public data (the Food Standards Agency business type, or the OpenStreetMap shop, amenity or craft tag), mapped to one of our 21 categories. If a business is in the wrong category, it can claim the listing and tell us, free.

Where does this list come from?

Names, addresses and categories come from the Food Standards Agency's Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (Open Government Licence v3.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). We publish nothing else unless the business gives it to us.
