About this page

How many things to do are listed in Lincolnshire?

89 listings are live today across the 22 town areas we cover, out of 179 on our schedule. The list refreshes from the live data every day.

How are businesses put in the Things to do category?

Things to do covers kids' play areas and soft play, trampoline parks, bowling, cinemas, theatres, museums and heritage centres, swimming pools and leisure centres, golf and sports clubs, attractions, escape rooms, karting and days out. We move a business here from the public data's own type (often restaurant or pub) when its name makes the activity clear, for example a play centre, a bowling alley or a golf club. Gyms stay under Sport & leisure; village halls and social clubs stay under Halls, clubs & venues. If a business is in the wrong category, it can claim the listing and tell us, free.

Where does this list come from?

Names, addresses and categories come from the Food Standards Agency's Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (Open Government Licence v3.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). We publish nothing else unless the business gives it to us.
