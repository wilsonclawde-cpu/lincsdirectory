Car air-con regas or repair in Woodhall Spa: the basics

What the job involves

If the air-con blows warm it has usually lost refrigerant through a slow leak, or the compressor or a sensor has failed. A regas recovers the old gas, checks for leaks, refills to the correct weight and adds lubricant. A smell from the vents is often bacteria in the evaporator, treated with an antibacterial clean.

Safety and good to know

About car air-con regas or repair in Woodhall Spa

How often should car air-con be regassed?

Manufacturers often suggest every two to three years, as systems lose a little gas over time. If it stops working suddenly there is probably a leak or a fault rather than normal loss.
