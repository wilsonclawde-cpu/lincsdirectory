Hair colour and styling in Louth: the basics

What the job involves

Colour services range from root touch-ups and full colour to highlights, balayage and colour correction, which can take several hours. Styling covers blow-dries, up-dos and occasion hair. Extensions and chemical treatments need a consultation first.

Safety and good to know

A skin patch test before colouring is standard practice because reactions to hair dye can be serious; don't skip it. LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote.

About hair colour and styling in Louth

Why do I need a patch test?

Hair dyes can cause allergic reactions, occasionally severe. Salons apply a small amount of the product behind the ear or on the arm a day or two before, to check your skin doesn't react.
