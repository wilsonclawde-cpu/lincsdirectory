Unblock a sink, bath or toilet in Lincoln: the basics

What the job involves

Sinks and showers block with grease, hair and soap; toilets block with too much paper or things that shouldn't be flushed (wipes, cotton buds, sanitary products). A plumber will clear the trap or waste pipe with a plunger, hand auger or small machine, and check the pipe falls correctly if it keeps happening.

Safety and good to know

Don't mix drain chemicals, and tell the plumber if you have poured any down. If a toilet is overflowing, close the lid, stop flushing and turn off the cistern's isolation valve if it has one. LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote.

About unblock a sink, bath or toilet in Lincoln

Why does my sink keep blocking?

Usually fat and food in a kitchen sink, or hair and soap in a bathroom. A plumber can clear it and check the trap and pipe run; using a sink strainer and never pouring fat down the sink keeps it clear for longer.
