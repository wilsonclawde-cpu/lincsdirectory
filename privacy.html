Privacy notice

Last updated: 7 October 2026. This explains how TAG Sleaford Ltd, trading as LincsDirectory, uses personal information.

The business directory

How listings are built

We build listings from two public datasets: the Food Standards Agency's Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (FHRS) data, published under the Open Government Licence v3.0, and OpenStreetMap, published by its contributors under the Open Database Licence. From these we publish only a business name, address (or, where OpenStreetMap records a business without an address, the nearest street and village taken from the map, marked "near"), postcode, category, map coordinates and, where the OpenStreetMap record carries one, the business phone number. We do not publish ratings, email addresses, opening hours or any other detail from public data.

For a sole trader or partnership, a business name and trading address can be personal information. We leave out records that appear to be private homes (for example home-based or mobile caterers, records with no premises address, flats, and names that look like an individual's name without a business name), and we leave out schools, hospitals, care homes, childcare and similar premises. We only list businesses in the seven Lincolnshire districts: Boston, East Lindsey, Lincoln, North Kesteven, South Holland, South Kesteven and West Lindsey.

Why we are allowed to do this

Our lawful basis is legitimate interests: ours in running a useful local directory, and those of local people who want to find businesses and of businesses who want to be found. The information is already public, is limited to what a customer would see on the shop front, and every business has an easy way to correct or remove it. We have balanced this against the rights of the people concerned and will reconsider for any individual who objects.

Removal and correction

Any business or person can ask us to correct or remove a listing at any time, free, using the claim form, by email to hello@lincsdirectory.co.uk, by phone on 01522 424963, or by post. We act promptly and keep a record of removed businesses so they are not re-added when the public data is refreshed.

Claimed and Premium listings

When you claim a listing, we ask for your name, role, email address and phone number so we can check you are connected to the business and deal with your request. On a claimed or owner-added free listing we publish the business details you ask us to publish: a one-line description, up to 3 service bullets and a short description, all read by a person before they appear. On a Premium listing we also publish the photo, website, phone number, opening hours and longer description you give us, on your profile page and in search results. We process this to perform our contract with you.

Ratings

When you rate a business we receive the star rating, your comment if you leave one, your first name and email address if you choose to give them, the listing you rated, and the fact that you ticked the box confirming you have used the business. We use this to check and, if approved, publish the rating (legitimate interests: honest, useful information about local businesses). We publish only the stars, the comment, the date and the first name you gave; we never publish your email address, and we use it only if we need to check something about your rating. A person reads every rating before it appears. We keep unpublished ratings, including ones we decline, for up to 12 months; published ratings stay until they are removed, which you can ask for at any time. If a business disputes a rating we may tell it the date and the first name shown, never your email address.

Suggestions from the public

Anyone can suggest a business for the directory. We keep what you submit: the business details you give us, who you say you are in relation to the business, and your name and email address if you choose to leave them. We use this to check the business against public records and, if it qualifies, add a free listing of its name, address and category. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (keeping the directory complete and accurate). Suggestions are never published automatically; a person checks each one. We do not publish the suggester's name or contact details, and we do not publish a business phone number or website from a suggestion unless the business confirms them. If you leave an email address we use it only to tell you when the listing is added. We keep suggestions for up to 12 months and delete them on request.

"Get local help" job requests

When you send a job request we collect what you type: the job and your description of it, your town and postcode (the first part is enough), how urgent it is, your name, and a phone number and/or email address. We use this to pass your request to listed local businesses that cover your area and do that kind of work, so that they can contact you about it. If you send the request from a business's own listing (the "Request a quote" button), the form also records that business's name and listing reference: the request still comes to LincsDirectory first, and we pass it on to listed businesses that cover your job and town, Promoted businesses first and free listings only if no Promoted business covers it, which can include the business you asked about, as the consent sentence on the form says. We do this only with the consent you give by ticking the box on the form; without it we don't send the form. A person reads each request before anything is shared; nothing is forwarded automatically, and we don't sell requests or share them with anyone else. The businesses we pass it to are responsible for how they handle your details once they have them. We keep requests for up to 12 months so we can answer questions about them, then delete them; ask at any time and we will delete yours sooner. The form is delivered by Web3Forms (see Forms below).

Tradespeople and service sign-ups

When you list your business through the For businesses page we collect the business name, a phone number, your email address, your website if you give one, and the plan you chose. We then look at your website and public records to work out your category, the jobs you do and the towns you cover; if you work from home we publish the towns you cover and never a home address. We publish the business details on the listing and job pages; your email is not published, and your phone number and website are published only on a Premium listing. Anything you tell us about registrations or credentials is shown as supplied by you and marked as unverified. We use your contact details to deal with your listing and to pass you customer requests for the jobs and towns you cover (contract, and legitimate interests). You can change or remove the listing at any time through the claim form.

Contacting businesses about the directory

We may write to or phone a listed business to tell it about its listing and the Premium option (legitimate interests). We follow the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations: we send marketing email only to corporate bodies such as limited companies and LLPs, always identifying ourselves and giving a simple way to opt out; we do not send unsolicited marketing email to sole traders or partnerships without their prior consent, and we contact them by post or phone instead; and we check the Telephone Preference Service before marketing calls. The public data we use contains no email addresses and we never scrape them. Ask us to stop at any time and we will add you to our do-not-contact list.

Forms on this site

When you send the claim, removal, suggestion, rating, job-request, business sign-up or advertising form we collect what you type: typically your name, email address or phone number, business name, website and your message. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-handling provider acting as our processor, which passes your message to our email inbox and does not use it for anything else. We use your details to deal with your request (contract, or legitimate interests for enquiries) and keep them for as long as the listing or enquiry is live, then for up to 6 years where there is a payment record (legal obligation). Opt-out and removal requests are kept indefinitely so that we honour them.

"Near me" and your location

If you use the "near me" feature, your browser asks your permission and then works out distances on your device. Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else. The map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which sees the map area you are viewing in the same way as any map website.

Payments

A Promoted listing is a monthly subscription taken through Stripe, our payment provider. The subscription is billed by TAG Sleaford Ltd, trading as LincsDirectory, whose name appears on the Stripe checkout page and on your receipts (see the terms). Stripe handles your card or wallet details (such as Apple Pay) on its own checkout page; we never see or store card numbers. Stripe sends us your name, your business name, your email address and the subscription status (started, paid, cancelled). We match a payment to a listing by the email address you give on the sign-up or claim form; please use the same one for both. We use this to switch the listing on and off and to answer questions about payments (contract, and legal obligation for payment records, kept for 6 years). Stripe acts as an independent controller for its own fraud-prevention and legal purposes and its checkout page may set cookies, as described in its own privacy notice. You can cancel from the link in Stripe's receipt emails or by emailing hello@lincsdirectory.co.uk.
