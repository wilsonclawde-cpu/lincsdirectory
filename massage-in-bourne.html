Massage and spa in Bourne: the basics

What the job involves

Massage therapists offer relaxation, deep tissue and sports massage, and spas combine treatments with pool and sauna facilities. Sports massage therapists work on specific muscle problems; some are also physiotherapists. Check the therapist's training and insurance.

Safety and good to know

Massage is not a treatment for medical conditions; see a GP or physiotherapist for pain that persists or is severe. LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote.

About massage and spa in Bourne

What is the difference between sports massage and deep tissue?

Both work firmly into the muscles. Sports massage focuses on a particular problem or activity and may include stretching and advice; deep tissue is a firm general treatment for tension.
