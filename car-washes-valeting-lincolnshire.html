How many car washes and valeters are listed in Lincolnshire?

How are businesses put in the car washes & valeting category?

From the business type recorded in the public data (the Food Standards Agency business type, or the OpenStreetMap shop, amenity or craft tag), mapped to one of our 35 categories, plus name-based rules for things like play centres and bowling alleys that the public data files under restaurants. If a business is in the wrong category, it can claim the listing and tell us, free.

Where does this list come from?
