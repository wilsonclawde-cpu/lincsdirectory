Which places count as Louth?

The Louth area covers the town itself plus the LN11, DN36 and DN37 postcode districts and villages such as Holton le Clay, North Thoresby and Tetney, following the way the public records are addressed. If you think a business is in the wrong town, claim the listing and tell us.

Where does this list come from?
