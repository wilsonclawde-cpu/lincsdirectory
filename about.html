About

A directory that starts complete and gets better.

Most local directories start empty and ask businesses to fill them. LincsDirectory starts with every Lincolnshire business we can find in public records, so people can use it from day one, and then hands each business the keys to its own listing.

How it works

We take two public datasets, the Food Standards Agency's Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (published under the Open Government Licence v3.0) and OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), and publish nothing more than a business name, address, category and map pin. New listings go live every day. Any business can claim its listing free and correct it, or ask us to remove it. Anyone can suggest a business we've missed; we check it before it goes live.

Businesses that want more can get a Promoted listing for £2.49 a month: their own profile page with their logo, photos, a full description and service points, a prominent link to their website, phone number and opening hours, first place in search results and on job pages for their town and category with a Premium tag, and a free local business report from Aide when they start. It is billed monthly through Stripe and can be cancelled at any time (who bills you is set out in the terms). Promoted (Premium) listings are paid placements and appear first; they are always marked.

Where to, what's broken, what do you need

Most people don't arrive looking for a category; they arrive with a job or an outing in mind. So the site has three doors: Where to? for things to do, places to eat and drink, shops and days out; What's broken? for repairs and fixes; and What do you need? for things to get done. Each job has a plain-English guide we wrote from public guidance (what's involved, what to ask a pro, what to have ready, the safety notes that matter) and a list of local businesses in the matching categories. You can also send a Get local help request: a person at LincsDirectory reads it and passes it, with your consent, to listed businesses that cover your area. We are an introducer, not a contractor, and we don't vet or endorse businesses: always check credentials and get a written quote. Tradespeople and service businesses can list the jobs they do and the towns they cover, free, with the option of a Promoted listing for £2.49 a month.

Ratings

Anyone can rate a business from its listing: one to five stars and, if they like, a short comment. A person reads every rating before it appears, so nothing is published automatically and we never invent ratings or counts; where a business has no approved ratings yet, we say so. We only publish genuine opinions from people who have used the business, we do not pay for ratings, offer incentives or accept payment to remove them, and we may remove anything abusive, defamatory, off-topic or that looks fake. Businesses can reply to a rating, or ask us to look at one, through the claim form. The full ratings policy is in our terms.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish email addresses, opening hours or anything beyond name, address, category and (where OpenStreetMap records it) a business phone number, unless the business gives it to us.
  • We don't vet, license or endorse businesses, and we don't guarantee any work. We tell visitors to check credentials and get a written quote.
  • We don't set cookies or run analytics trackers. The "near me" feature works entirely in your browser; your location is never sent to us.
  • We don't invent reviews, ratings or testimonials. Every rating shown was sent by a visitor and checked by a person; counts shown on the site are computed from the listings and ratings that are live.
  • We don't sell your information.

Corrections and removals

Public records lag behind reality: businesses close, move and change hands. If a listing is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it. If you want your business off the directory, ask and we'll remove it promptly and keep it off.
