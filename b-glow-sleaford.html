About GLOW Sleaford

GLOW Sleaford is a superfood cafe run by Courtney on the ground floor of Kesteven Business Centre. Everything is blended fresh to order: acai bowls, protein shakes, smoothies, iced and hot coffee, ceremonial matcha, juices and wellness shots, with no refined sugar and vegan options throughout.

The menu is built around functional ingredients, so every bowl, shake and shot carries benefit tags such as energy, protein, gut health, immunity, focus and skin. House-made syrups and cold foams, seasonal specials and autumn favourites change through the year.

There is also a food counter with protein salads, overnight oats, snacks and cakes and bakes. Walk in during the week, order for cold delivery across Sleaford through Uber Eats, or call about catering for your team, meeting or event.
