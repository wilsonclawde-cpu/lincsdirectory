Local requests

When someone sends a "Get local help" request for a job you do in a town you cover, we pass it to you by email or phone. You deal with the customer directly. We make no promises about how many you'll get.

We don't vet businesses and we don't tell customers we do. Credentials you give us (for example a Gas Safe registration number) are shown only if you supply them and are marked as supplied by you, not verified by us. Customers are told to check before they book.

How it works

  • Send the form. A person checks the business is real (Companies House, your website, public records) and adds the listing, usually within a few days.
  • Your listing appears on the job pages you ticked, for the towns you ticked, and in search.
  • Work from home? Choose "I cover an area": we show the towns you cover, never your home address.
  • Promoted: you subscribe for £1.99 a month on the claim page (Apple Pay, Google Pay or card through Stripe), then send us your photo and description. A person switches your profile page on within one working day of the first payment.
  • Change or remove your listing any time through the claim form.
