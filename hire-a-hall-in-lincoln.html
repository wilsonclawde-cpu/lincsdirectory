Hire a hall or venue in Lincoln: the basics

What the job involves

Village and community halls, pubs with function rooms, hotels and sports clubs hire rooms for parties, wakes, classes and meetings. Halls usually have a kitchen, tables and chairs and a hire fee by the hour or session; pubs and hotels may offer the room free with a minimum spend on food and drink.

About hire a hall or venue in Lincoln

Can we bring our own drink to a hired hall?

Many village halls allow it for private parties where drink isn't sold. Selling alcohol needs a licence or a temporary event notice from the council, which the hall committee will explain.
