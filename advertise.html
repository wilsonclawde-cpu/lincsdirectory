Advertise with us

Be seen by people who are already looking.

LincsDirectory visitors arrive with a need: a plumber in Boston, a Chinese in Sleaford, an office in Lincoln. A labelled advert on the right page puts you in front of them at that moment.

Ad slots

Every advert is clearly labelled "Advertisement". Pricing is on enquiry and depends on the slot, the towns or categories you want, and how long you run.

Home-page strip

A card in the advert strip on the home page, seen by everyone who lands on LincsDirectory. Headline, two lines of text, logo mark and a button.

Advertisement

Search-results slot

A card beside the results and map. Can be shown only for the towns or categories you choose, so a Grantham garage reaches Grantham motorists.

Advertisement

Category sponsor

Your advert on every search in one category county-wide, for example all hair and beauty searches, with a "Sponsored" label.

What you get

  • Your advert designed to match the site, with your logo, message and link, labelled as an advertisement.
  • A plain monthly note of how many pages your advert appeared on and how many people clicked it. We do not track visitors with cookies, so the figures are simple counts.
  • Changes to your copy whenever you need them, at no extra cost.
  • A Featured listing for your business while your advert runs, if you don't already have one.

Adverts must be for a lawful business or service relevant to Lincolnshire. We don't run adverts for gambling, adult services or anything that competes directly with the free listing promise of the directory.

Ask for a price

Tell us what you do and who you want to reach. We'll reply by email, usually within one working day, with options and prices. Or phone 01522 424963.
