LincsDirectory visitors arrive with a need: a plumber in Boston, a Chinese in Sleaford, an office in Lincoln. A Premium slot on the right page puts you in front of them at that moment.

Every paid placement carries a small "Premium listing" tag, so readers always know. Pricing is on enquiry and depends on the slot, the towns or categories you want, and how long you run.

What you get

  • Your card designed to match the site, with your logo, message and link, marked as a Premium listing.
  • A plain monthly note of how many pages your card appeared on and how many people clicked it. We do not track visitors with cookies, so the figures are simple counts.
  • Changes to your copy whenever you need them, at no extra cost.
  • A Promoted listing (profile page, first in results, normally £2.49 a month) for your business while your slot runs, if you don't already have one. See what a Promoted listing includes.
Just want your own business found? You may not need a slot. A Promoted listing is £2.49 a month: first in results with a Premium tag, your own page, photo, website link, phone, hours and description. Get Promoted or join free.

Placements must be for a lawful business or service relevant to Lincolnshire. We don't run placements for gambling, adult services or anything that competes directly with the free listing promise of the directory.
