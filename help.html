When?

A person at LincsDirectory reads every request.

LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote. In an emergency call 999.

Gas smell? Leave the property, don't use flames or switches, open doors and windows and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999. Danger to life? Call 999.

What happens next

  • We read your request (a person, not a machine) and pass it, with the contact details you gave, to listed businesses that cover your town and do this kind of work. Sent it from a business's own listing ("Request a quote")? It still comes to us first. We pass requests to Promoted businesses that cover the job and town first, and to free listings only if no Promoted business covers it.
  • They may contact you directly by phone or email. We don't promise how many will reply or how quickly; it depends on who covers your area.
  • You deal with the business directly. LincsDirectory is an introducer, not a contractor: we don't vet or endorse businesses, so check credentials and get a written quote.
  • We keep your request for up to 12 months and delete it on request. Details are in the privacy notice.
