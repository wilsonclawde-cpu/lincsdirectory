About this page

How many businesses in Skegness are listed?

Which places count as Skegness?

The Skegness area covers the town itself plus the PE24 and PE25 postcode districts and villages such as Burgh le Marsh, Chapel St Leonards and Ingoldmells, following the way the public records are addressed. If you think a business is in the wrong town, claim the listing and tell us.

Where does this list come from?

Names, addresses and categories come from the Food Standards Agency's Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (Open Government Licence v3.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). We publish nothing else unless the business gives it to us. Businesses can claim, correct or remove their listing free.
