Hire a hall or venue: the basics

What the job involves

Village and community halls, pubs with function rooms, hotels and sports clubs hire rooms for parties, wakes, classes and meetings. Halls usually have a kitchen, tables and chairs and a hire fee by the hour or session; pubs and hotels may offer the room free with a minimum spend on food and drink.

What to ask a pro, and what to have ready

Ask about capacity, kitchen and bar arrangements (some halls need a temporary event notice for alcohol sales), parking, accessibility, set-up and clearing time, deposit and whether you need your own insurance.

Safety and good to know

LincsDirectory doesn't vet or endorse businesses — always check credentials and get a written quote.

Good to know

hall hire often includes a damage deposit and a requirement to leave the hall as you found it, including bins. Check the end time and whether music must stop at a set hour. Many halls have a hearing loop, step-free access and a baby-changing area; ask if you need them.

Can we bring our own drink to a hired hall?

Many village halls allow it for private parties where drink isn't sold. Selling alcohol needs a licence or a temporary event notice from the council, which the hall committee will explain.
