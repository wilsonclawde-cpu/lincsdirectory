Phone screen repair in Sleaford: the basics

What the job involves

Phone repair shops replace cracked screens, worn batteries, charging ports and cameras, often while you wait. Screens come as original or compatible parts at different prices and quality; some phones show a warning or lose a feature with a non-original part.

Will a new screen affect Face ID or fingerprint unlock?
